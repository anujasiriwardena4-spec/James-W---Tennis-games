          About BASELINE

          BASELINE is a free tennis career simulator you play in the browser — no download, no sign-up. You build a player by spinning a wheel of real tour names and legends, taking exactly one of the eight ratings from each name that comes up: Sinner's backhand, Isner's serve, Nadal's stamina. Each name is used once, so every build is a different compromise.

          From there you play the season. Seventeen events — four Grand Slams, the Masters 1000s, the ATP 500s and the Tour Finals — on hard, clay, grass and indoor courts, each with its own scoring model, so serve-bots really do prefer grass and grinders really do prefer clay. Matches are simulated point by point, ranking points sit in a 52-week rolling table, and you can either sim a match or play it yourself game by game, choosing how you serve and how you return.

          Off court there is a career to manage: prize money, fatigue and injury risk, a lifestyle economy where net worth buys a house, a car and a coaching team, and finally retirement with a legacy verdict on everything you won. Three save slots run side by side. There is also a local 2v2 doubles mode for four players on one screen, quick singles exhibitions, and a 30-question tennis trivia round.

          How to play

          1. Name your player and pick a country and playing hand.
          2. Spin for eight skills. You get three re-spins — use them when the wheel lands badly.
          3. Take exactly one skill per player. Nobody gets used twice.
          4. Play the calendar, climb the rankings, bank prize money, manage fatigue.
          5. Retire when you're ready and read the verdict on your career.

          Free to play in any modern browser. Player names are used to describe playing styles in a fan-made simulation; this project is not affiliated with the ATP, the WTA, any tournament, or any player.
